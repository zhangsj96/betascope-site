Shadow pattern speed

Ωs > 0 (prograde): the shadow turns in the same sense as the disk. Ωs < 0 (retrograde): the opposite sense. β′ = β(1 − Ωs/Ω), Ps = 2π/|Ωs|. Retrograde shadows keep the stationary lag direction (β′ > β). Prograde shadows faster than the gas (Ωs > Ω) give β′ < 0, and the lag reverses.
On the diagram: hover anywhere (either panel) to preview the 1D curves at that β′; click the upper panel to set the disk-frame β (open diamond); drag the filled diamond along the coloured track, or scroll with the cursor near it, to change Ωs — one gesture runs retrograde → stationary → prograde → corotation → overtaking.
